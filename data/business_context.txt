Company: AuroraFlow is a B2B SaaS platform for procurement analytics and contract lifecycle automation.
Target customers: Mid-market and enterprise companies with annual procurement spend above 20 million USD.
Primary buyers: CFO, Head of Procurement, and VP Operations.
Business value proposition: Reduce procurement leakage, shorten contract cycle time, and improve supplier compliance.
Core product modules: Spend Intelligence, Contract Copilot, Supplier Risk Monitor, Approval Workflow Engine.
Spend Intelligence detects maverick spend by comparing PO lines against approved supplier catalogs.
Contract Copilot drafts redline suggestions based on approved legal playbooks and clause templates.
Supplier Risk Monitor aggregates sanctions data, ESG signals, and delivery reliability metrics.
Approval Workflow Engine enforces four-eyes approval for purchases above policy thresholds.
Main KPI: Procurement Leakage Rate equals off-policy spend divided by total managed spend.
Main KPI target: Procurement Leakage Rate should stay below 2.5 percent for enterprise tier customers.
Main KPI: Contract Cycle Time is measured from request submission to final signature.
Main KPI target: Contract Cycle Time should be below 14 calendar days for standard MSA renewals.
Main KPI: Supplier On-Time Delivery is calculated as on-time shipments divided by total shipments.
Main KPI target: Supplier On-Time Delivery should remain above 96 percent.
Pricing model: Annual subscription plus usage-based fee for document processing volume.
Starter plan: 60,000 USD per year, up to 50 users, one ERP integration.
Growth plan: 140,000 USD per year, up to 200 users, three ERP integrations, advanced dashboards.
Enterprise plan: Custom pricing, unlimited users, SSO, private deployment options.
Overage policy: Document processing above plan limit is billed at 0.08 USD per page.
SLA: 99.9 percent monthly API uptime for Growth and Enterprise plans.
SLA service credits: 10 percent credit for uptime below 99.9 and 25 percent credit for uptime below 99.5.
Support policy: P1 incidents response time is 15 minutes, P2 is 1 hour, P3 is 4 business hours.
Incident communication: Status page update every 30 minutes during active P1 incidents.
Data retention: Operational logs retained for 365 days; model interaction traces retained for 90 days.
Security baseline: SOC 2 Type II, encryption at rest with AES-256, and TLS 1.2+ in transit.
PII policy: Personal data in prompts must be masked before persistence in analytics logs.
Access control: RBAC roles are Viewer, Analyst, Manager, and Admin.
Manager role can approve purchases up to 250,000 USD; Admin has no approval cap.
Common objection: Finance teams worry about hallucinated legal language.
Objection handling: Contract Copilot suggestions are always linked to approved clause sources and confidence scores.
Pilot playbook: 6-week pilot with success criteria on leakage reduction and cycle-time improvement.
Pilot success gate: At least 15 percent reduction in leakage and 20 percent faster contract turnaround.
Renewal strategy: Present ROI report at day 75 with before/after KPI comparison.
Upsell trigger: If monthly processed pages exceed 85 percent of quota for two consecutive months.
Churn risk signal: Three consecutive weeks with fewer than five active approvers in workflow module.
Sales motion: Land with Spend Intelligence, expand into Contract Copilot by quarter two.
Implementation timeline: Typical go-live in 4 to 8 weeks depending on ERP complexity.
Integration stack: SAP S/4HANA, Oracle NetSuite, Coupa, and Workday supported.
Known product limitation: OCR quality degrades on low-resolution scanned contracts under 150 DPI.
Mitigation: Pre-processing with document enhancement is recommended before ingestion.
